Who Is Using GeoSentry?

Select your authorized institutional role or citizen persona below to activate customized dashboards, permissions, and rapid emergency tools.

District Administrations

District Magistrates, Collectors, and Revenue Officers managing regional emergency mitigation.

  • District-level hazard prioritization
  • Evacuation zone sanctioning
  • Emergency fund allocation

Disaster Management Authorities

NDMA, SDMA, and DDMA state and national emergency coordination headquarters.

  • Multi-channel alert broadcasts (SMS/Push)
  • Early warning threshold monitoring
  • Inter-agency resource coordination

State Government Agencies

Departments of Relief, Rehabilitation, Forest, Geology, and Mining.

  • State-wide risk mapping & GIS feeds
  • Satellite Earth Observation tracking
  • Long-term slope stabilization planning

Emergency Response Teams

NDRF, SDRF, Fire & Rescue Services, and 108 Emergency Medical Services.

  • Trauma hospital bed live tracking
  • Fast rescue route navigation
  • Ground deployment telemetry

Road & Infrastructure Authorities

NHAI, State PWD, Border Roads Organisation (BRO), and Indian Railways.

  • Mountain highway blockage alerts (NH-48, NH-10)
  • Debris clearance machinery dispatch
  • Critical bridge & tunnel vulnerability checks

Local Administrations

Gram Panchayats, Block Development Officers, and Municipal Councils.

  • Village-level shelter activation
  • Direct local siren & PA warnings
  • Community headcount & vulnerable register

Field Officials & Validators

Ground verification officers, geologists, and middleman verifiers.

  • Review & validate citizen field reports
  • Ground truth crack measurements
  • Trigger automated Celery risk recomputations

Remote & Vulnerable Communities

Tribal settlements, isolated hill hamlets, and high-slope agrarian villages.

  • Multi-lingual voice bot (STT & TTS)
  • Offline-ready emergency alert SMS
  • Nearest medical clinic & shelter path

Citizens Living in Landslide Areas

Local residents, mountain commuters, hillside tourists, and local businesses.

  • Submit photo/video reports of cracks
  • View local danger zone warnings
  • Access nearest rescue hospitals & 108
